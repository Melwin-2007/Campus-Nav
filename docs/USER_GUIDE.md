# Campus Navigator — User Guide

Welcome to **Campus Navigator**! This guide explains how to find campus buildings, get walking directions, use the accessibility mode, track your live location, and scan QR codes.

---

## 1. Exploring the Campus Map

### Moving Around the Map
- **Desktop**: Click and drag with your mouse to pan. Use your mouse scroll wheel or the `+` / `-` buttons to zoom in and out.
- **Mobile**: Swipe with one finger to pan. Pinch with two fingers to zoom.
- **Reset View**: Tap the **Compass** icon in the top-right corner at any time to return to the campus center.

### Finding Buildings & Facilities
- Each building or facility on campus is marked with a colorful circular pin.
- Each pin has a distinct color and icon representing its category (e.g., Blue for Academic Buildings, Green for Libraries, Orange for Food & Dining, Rose for Medical).
- Tap or click any marker to open its **Details Card**.

---

## 2. Searching for Places

1. Tap the **Search Bar** at the top of the screen.
2. Start typing a building name, department, facility, or keyword (for example, type `lib` to find the Central Library, or `canteen` to find dining options).
3. Live suggestions will appear instantly below the search bar.
4. Tap any suggestion to center the map on that place and open its details.

### Category Filters
Under the search bar, you will see category filter pills (such as *Academic*, *Library*, *Food*, *Sports*).
- Tap a category pill to show only buildings belonging to that category on the map.
- Tap the active pill again or tap **All** to clear the filter.

---

## 3. Getting Walking Directions

1. Open any building's details and tap **Get Directions**, or tap the **Directions** icon in the bottom menu.
2. Select your **Start Location** and **Destination**:
   - You can choose from the dropdown list.
   - Or select **"My Location"** to navigate directly from where you are standing.
   - Tap the **Swap** button ($\updownarrow$) to invert your start and destination.
3. **Step-Free / Accessible Routes**:
   - Toggle the **Accessible Route (No Stairs)** switch to ensure the path avoids stairs, steep steps, and unpaved terrain. The app will calculate a route using ramps, wide walkways, and elevators.
4. Tap **Find Route**:
   - An animated teal line with a white outline will draw the route along real campus paths.
   - A summary box shows total walking distance (in meters) and estimated walking time (in minutes).
   - Below the summary, follow step-by-step turn directions (e.g., *Head north on Main Avenue*, *Turn right at Fountain Plaza*, *Arrive at Central Library*).
   - Tap any step to zoom the map directly to that junction.

---

## 4. Using GPS Live Location

1. Tap the **Navigation Arrow** icon in the top-right floating toolbar.
2. When prompted by your browser, tap **Allow** to enable location services.
3. A glowing teal dot with an accuracy circle will appear on the map showing your current position.
4. As you walk, the dot moves with you in real time.
5. If you are outside the campus boundary (more than 300 meters away), the app will display a friendly banner: *"You appear to be away from campus. Please select your starting point manually."*

---

## 5. Scanning Campus QR Codes

Campus buildings have QR code posters posted at their main entrances.
1. Tap the **Scan QR** button in the bottom menu bar or in the search bar.
2. Point your phone camera at the QR poster.
3. The app will recognize the code instantly, open the building's details, and show a **"Directions From Here"** button so you can navigate anywhere on campus with one tap.
4. If you don't want to use the camera, you can also type the code manually (e.g., `LOC-001`).

---

## 6. Light and Dark Modes

Tap the **Sun / Moon** icon in the top-right toolbar to toggle between clean day mode and high-contrast dark mode for night-time navigation.
